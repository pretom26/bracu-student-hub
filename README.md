
# BRACU Student Hub

**A student-focused platform for academics, campus life, and career opportunities.**

*Developed as a CSE470: Software Engineering course project at BRAC University.*

[View the GitHub Repository](https://github.com/pretom26/bracu-student-hub)

---

## About the Project

University life involves much more than attending classes. Students have to manage assignments, prepare for exams, keep track of their academic progress, coordinate with classmates, manage expenses, and explore opportunities beyond the classroom.

Most of these activities are handled through different websites, applications, spreadsheets, or social media groups. Switching between multiple platforms can make everyday university responsibilities unnecessarily complicated.

**BRACU Student Hub** was developed to bring these essential services together in one convenient platform.

Built primarily for students of BRAC University, the application combines academic management, campus services, student collaboration, and career development tools within a unified web-based environment.

Our goal is simple: to make university life more organized, accessible, and manageable.

The project was developed using the **MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js)** as part of our CSE470 Software Engineering coursework, following an Agile development approach across multiple sprints.

> **Project Status:** Academic software engineering project. The repository contains multiple frontend and backend development versions. Features documented here reflect the project's Software Requirements Specification (SRS) and implementation reports. Some modules may require further integration to operate together.

## Project Objectives

BRACU Student Hub was designed with the following objectives:

- Provide a centralized platform for academic and campus-related activities.
- Help students organize courses, assignments, examinations, and schedules.
- Make academic progress tracking and graduation planning easier.
- Encourage student collaboration through study groups and shared resources.
- Improve access to important campus information and facilities.
- Connect students with internships, scholarships, and part-time employment opportunities.
- Support everyday student needs, including financial planning and transportation coordination.
- Provide a secure and user-friendly experience for students and administrators.

## Key Features

The application includes features across several major categories.

### 1. Academic Management

#### CGPA Calculator

The CGPA Calculator helps students monitor their academic performance without manually calculating grade points.

Students can:

- Enter course grades and credit hours.
- Calculate semester-wise GPA.
- Calculate cumulative CGPA.
- Save semester results for future reference.
- Account for repeated courses when calculating academic progress.

The calculator is also connected with the Graduation Planner to support more convenient academic tracking.

#### Graduation Planner

The Graduation Planner provides students with a clearer understanding of their progress toward completing their degree.

Features include:

- Tracking completed, ongoing, and remaining courses.
- Viewing progress toward graduation requirements.
- Organizing courses by academic categories.
- Planning courses for upcoming semesters.
- Integrating academic results with the CGPA Calculator.
- Monitoring completed credits and remaining requirements.

This feature helps students make informed decisions about their future semesters.

#### Class Routine Manager

The Class Routine Manager provides a structured timetable for organizing regular academic activities.

Students can:

- Create and manage class schedules.
- Organize classes and labs by day and time.
- View weekly routines.
- Identify scheduling conflicts.
- Keep track of upcoming academic sessions.

#### Assignment and Exam Deadline Tracker

Keeping track of multiple deadlines can become challenging during a semester.

The Deadline Tracker helps students organize their assignments and examinations in one place.

Features include:

- Adding assignments and exam schedules.
- Recording course information and deadlines.
- Displaying live countdown timers.
- Editing or deleting existing deadlines.
- Filtering deadlines by course.
- Organizing assessments by category.

#### Academic Calendar

The Academic Calendar provides an interactive environment for managing university events and personal academic schedules.

Students can:

- View events in a calendar interface.
- Create personal events.
- Edit or remove existing personal events.
- Track university events and club activities.
- View examination schedules.
- Categorize events using different colors.
- View event details through interactive calendar controls.

Administrators can also publish university-wide events and announcements, allowing important updates to reach students through the same platform.

#### Free Lab Finder

Finding an available laboratory or study space can be difficult during busy university hours.

The Free Lab Finder helps students identify available labs by comparing requested time slots against scheduled lab usage.

Students can search for availability based on their preferred date and time.

### 2. Campus Life and Services

#### Cafeteria Menu and Reviews

The cafeteria module helps students explore available meals before visiting the campus cafeteria.

Features include:

- Viewing daily cafeteria menus.
- Browsing breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack options.
- Exploring weekly menu schedules.
- Viewing featured and highly rated food items.
- Submitting food ratings and reviews.
- Reading feedback submitted by other students.

Administrators can manage cafeteria items and menu information.

#### Textbook Exchange

The Textbook Exchange provides a convenient way for students to buy, sell, or exchange academic textbooks within the university community.

Students can:

- Create textbook listings.
- Include prices, conditions, and contact information.
- Specify pickup locations.
- Browse available books.
- Filter textbooks by relevant information.
- Save interesting listings to a favorites section.
- Manage their own listings.

This feature encourages the reuse of textbooks and makes academic resources more accessible.

#### Budget Manager

Managing personal finances is an important part of university life.

The Budget Manager helps students keep track of their income, expenses, and savings.

Features include:

- Recording daily financial transactions.
- Tracking monthly expenses.
- Setting spending limits and savings goals.
- Categorizing transactions.
- Viewing transaction history.
- Visualizing spending through a calendar.
- Reviewing financial summaries and spending patterns.

The Study Group Finder helps students connect with others studying similar courses.

Students can:

- Create course-specific study groups.
- Search for existing groups.
- Express interest in joining groups.
- Manage group memberships.
- Create public or private groups.
- Communicate with group members.
- Participate in group discussions.

The Transport Group Finder makes it easier for students to coordinate shared transportation.

Features include:

- Creating transportation groups.
- Searching for groups based on routes.
- Joining existing commuting groups.
- Managing group memberships.
- Setting group privacy preferences.
- Specifying member preferences where supported.
- Communicating through group messaging.

The feature is designed to make shared commuting more organized and convenient.

#### Course Content Sharing

The Course Content module allows students to share educational materials with their peers.

Students can:

- Upload lecture notes and study materials.
- Organize materials by course.
- Search for relevant academic resources.
- View shared content.
- Download available materials.
- Track their own submissions.

Uploaded materials go through an administrative approval process before becoming publicly available within the platform.

#### Academic Q&A Forum

The Q&A Forum provides a space for students to ask questions and help one another.

Students can:

- Post academic questions.
- Browse questions from other students.
- Submit answers.
- Search for relevant discussions.
- Participate in course-related conversations.

The objective is to encourage collaborative learning and make academic assistance more accessible.

#### Course Review System

The Course Review System allows students to share their experiences with different university courses.

Features include:

- Searching for courses.
- Submitting course reviews.
- Rating course content and difficulty.
- Providing feedback about the learning experience.
- Viewing reviews from other students.

- Reviewing submitted course materials.
- Moderating course reviews.

## Technology Stack

BRACU Student Hub was developed using the MERN stack and supporting web development technologies.

| Category | Technologies |
|----------|--------------|
| Frontend | React.js, JavaScript, HTML, CSS |
| Frontend Build Tool | Vite |
| Navigation | React Router |

| Backend Structure | Modular MVC-style architecture |
| Development Methodology | Agile, sprint-based development |
| Version Control | Git and GitHub |

### Why the MERN Stack?

The MERN stack was chosen because it provides a practical environment for developing a full-stack web application.

**MongoDB** allows flexible storage and management of application data.

**Express.js** provides the backend framework for managing API requests, authentication, and application logic.

**React.js** makes it possible to build interactive and reusable frontend components.

**Node.js** enables server-side development using JavaScript.

Together, these technologies support modular application development and integration between frontend interfaces and backend services.

## System Architecture

BRACU Student Hub follows a client-server architecture.

The frontend handles user interaction and presentation, while the backend manages application logic, authentication, database operations, and API communication.

```text
                 BRACU STUDENT HUB
                         |
                         v
                 Student / Admin
                         |
                         v
                 React.js Frontend
                         |
                         v
                  Axios / REST API
                         |
                         v
                Node.js + Express.js
                         |
             +-----------+-----------+
             |                       |
             v                       v
       Authentication          Business Logic
             |                       |
             +-----------+-----------+
                         |
                         v
                Mongoose Models
                         |
                         v
                   MongoDB
```

The architecture separates user interface components from backend functionality and database operations.

This approach makes individual features easier to maintain, improve, and extend.

## Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the project locally.

### Prerequisites

Make sure the following tools are installed:

- [Git](https://git-scm.com/)
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/)
- npm (included with Node.js)
- [MongoDB Community Server](https://www.mongodb.com/try/download/community) or [MongoDB Atlas](https://www.mongodb.com/atlas)
- A code editor such as [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/)

### Step 1: Clone the Repository

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/pretom26/bracu-student-hub.git
```

Navigate into the project directory:

```bash
cd bracu-student-hub
```

### Step 2: Choose the Application Directory

The repository contains multiple frontend and backend development versions.

For example, one application version is organized as:

```text
BracustudentHUBvite/
├── backend/
└── frontend-vite/
```

The following instructions use this application version.

Other modules are also available in the repository-level `backend/` and `frontend-vite/` directories.

### Step 3: Install Backend Dependencies

Navigate to the backend directory:

```bash
cd BracustudentHUBvite/backend
```

Install the required dependencies:

```bash
npm install
```

### Step 4: Configure Environment Variables

Create a `.env` file inside the backend directory.

Example:

```env
MONGODB_URI=mongodb://127.0.0.1:27017/bracu_student_hub
PORT=5000
NODE_ENV=development
```

If you are using MongoDB Atlas, replace `MONGODB_URI` with your Atlas database connection string.

Do not commit database passwords, API credentials, or `.env` files to GitHub.

### Step 5: Start the Backend

Run:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The backend is configured to run on:

```text
http://localhost:5000
```

An API health endpoint is available at:

```text
http://localhost:5000/api/health
```

### Step 6: Install Frontend Dependencies

Open another terminal and navigate to the frontend directory:

```bash
cd bracu-student-hub/BracustudentHUBvite/frontend-vite
```

If you are already inside the repository root, use:

```bash
cd BracustudentHUBvite/frontend-vite
```

Install the dependencies:

```bash
npm install
```

### Step 7: Configure the Frontend

Create a `.env` file inside the frontend directory if you need to specify the backend URL.

```env
VITE_API_URL=http://localhost:5000
```

### Step 8: Start the Frontend

Run:

```bash
npm run dev
```

Vite will display a local development URL, typically:

```text
http://localhost:5173
```

Open the URL in your browser to access the application.

You can then register or log in to explore the available features.

> **Note:** The setup instructions reflect the repository's available development scripts and configuration. A complete clean-install execution has not been independently verified. Because some modules are maintained in separate development versions, additional integration or sample data may be required.

## Development Methodology

BRACU Student Hub was developed using an **Agile, sprint-based software development approach**.

The project was divided into multiple development phases to make implementation, integration, and testing more manageable.

### Sprint 1: Core Functionality

The first sprint focused on establishing the foundation of the application.

Main features included:

- User authentication.
- CGPA Calculator.
- Academic Event Calendar.
- Assignment Deadline Tracker.
- Exam Countdown.
- Cafeteria Menu Display.

This sprint established the initial frontend and backend architecture, database connectivity, and core academic tools.

### Sprint 2: Feature Expansion

The second sprint focused on expanding the platform into a broader student management system.

Major additions included:

- Graduation Planner.
- Study Group Finder.
- Transport Group Finder.
- Class Routine Manager.
- Free Lab Finder.
- Budget Manager.
- Course Content Sharing.
- Course Review System.
- Additional campus and student services.

The focus was on improving collaboration, planning, and everyday student convenience.

### Sprint 3: Refinement and Extended Services

The later development phase focused on refining existing modules and extending the application's functionality.

Areas of work included:

- Improving existing interfaces.
- Extending student career services.
- Developing application management workflows.
- Improving administrative functionality.
- Strengthening validation and moderation.
- Refining integration between frontend and backend modules.

Throughout development, the team worked on maintaining a consistent user experience across the different sections of the platform.

## Challenges Faced

Developing an application with many interconnected features introduced several challenges.

### Managing a Large Project Scope

The application combines academic tools, campus services, collaboration features, and career resources.

Completing these modules within limited development time required careful planning and task distribution.

### Frontend and Backend Integration

Ensuring that independent frontend components communicated correctly with backend services required coordination and debugging.

### Real-Time Information Updates

Features such as exam countdowns and lab availability required careful handling of time-dependent data.

### Maintaining UI Consistency

Different team members worked on different modules.

Maintaining similar navigation patterns, visual styling, and user interactions across the application was an important consideration.

### Collaborative Development

Integrating independently developed features highlighted the importance of version control, clear communication, and consistent project organization.

These challenges helped the team gain practical experience in full-stack application development and collaborative software engineering.

## Known Limitations

BRACU Student Hub was developed as an academic project and is not intended to be considered production-ready in its current form.

Some limitations include:

- Features are distributed across multiple application versions.
- Additional integration may be required to combine all modules into one application.
- Database-dependent functionality requires valid configuration and data.
- Some features depend on information being manually added by users or administrators.
- Production security and deployment configurations require further review.
- Comprehensive automated testing has not been verified across the complete repository.

These limitations provide opportunities for future development and improvement.
